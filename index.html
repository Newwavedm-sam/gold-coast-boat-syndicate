How usage works

Fair access for everyone.

Each 20% share has access to up to roughly one fifth of the year, but the aim is not to divide the calendar into rigid blocks. We use a shared calendar, a 3-month booking window and simple usage tracking so the boat gets used as much as possible.

📅

3-month booking window.

Book up to three months ahead, keeping planning useful without locking the boat up a year in advance.

☀️

Up to 7 days at a time.

A share can book up to seven consecutive days. Longer trips simply need agreement from the other shareholders.

⚖️

Usage stays balanced.

If booking demand clashes, the share with fewer used days gets a slightly earlier booking opportunity.

🤝

Shared trips split the days.

If multiple shares use the boat together, the days are divided between those shares instead of counting the full trip against everyone.

Example

A long weekend together.

Share 2 + Share 4 take the boat away together from Friday to Monday. That is a 4-day booking, but because both ownership groups are using it together, the usage is split between them: 2 days charged to Share 2 and 2 days charged to Share 4.

If three shares went together, the same principle applies: the trip is divided proportionally between the participating shares. That encourages owners to actually use the boat together rather than penalising shared trips.

Five shares contributing $200/month gives the syndicate $12,000/year. Normal costs should use only part of that, with the balance staying in reserve. Fuel is user-pays.

Why keep a reserve?

The aim is to avoid every unexpected repair turning into an immediate cash call. The opening reserve plus unused monthly contributions provide breathing room for servicing, trailer work, insurance excesses and mechanical surprises.

The $200 monthly contribution is reviewed annually and can be reduced, increased or paused if the kitty becomes materially over- or under-funded.

How do shared trips work?

If two ownership groups take the boat away together for four days, two usage days are allocated to each share. Three participating shares would split the usage three ways.

Where is the boat kept?

The plan is dedicated boat storage in Broadbeach Waters immediately beside a private boat ramp, making access to the Broadwater much easier.

What if two shareholders want the same dates?

Usage history becomes the tie-breaker. The share that has used fewer days receives a slightly earlier booking opportunity for that period.
