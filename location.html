Weekend mission territory

The trailer keeps your
options open.

Because the syndicate owns the trailer as well as the boat, you are not limited to Broadbeach Waters. For an occasional full-day or weekend trip, suitable powered-boating destinations include Lake Maroon, Lake Moogerah, Somerset Dam and Lake Wivenhoe, subject to current local rules and conditions.

Worth knowing: nearby does not always mean suitable. Hinze Dam and Wyaralong are not wake-boat destinations, so they are not included in the pitch.

The towing vehicle

Do I need my own?

Ideally, members should have access to a suitable tow vehicle if they want complete flexibility.

There will often be a suitable towing vehicle at the Broadbeach Waters location, and Sam can usually help with launching or moving the boat when he is around. That assistance is a convenience, not a guaranteed service, so members should not rely on it always being available.

For normal local use, the private ramp right beside the storage location also keeps towing to an absolute minimum.

Why location matters

The easier it is,
the more it gets used.

A lot of boats get used less than their owners imagined because every outing becomes a major logistical exercise. Here, the boat is already stored close to the water, the private ramp is right there and the Gold Coast waterway network is on the doorstep.
