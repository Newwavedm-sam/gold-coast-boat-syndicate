The logistics

How a boating day actually works.

The practical part matters almost as much as the boat. The plan is to keep it in dedicated boat storage in Broadbeach Waters, right beside a private boat ramp, so owners do not need to find somewhere to keep a boat and trailer at home.

Shared ownership

And yes, you can use it together.

The system is deliberately designed so shareholders can organise trips together. If two shares take the boat away for a four-day long weekend, the four days are split between them: two usage days each. If three shares go together, usage is divided three ways.

That means inviting another ownership group along does not punish everyone with a full booking against their annual usage.
